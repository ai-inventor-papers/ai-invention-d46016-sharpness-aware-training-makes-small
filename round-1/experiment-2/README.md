## Layman Summary

We train the same small image classifier two ways on clothing photos and check whether the sharper-minimum training method makes its confidence more trustworthy, and whether that benefit comes from avoiding overfitting.

## Full Summary

Mechanism sub-experiment on whether Sharpness-Aware Minimization (SAM) calibration gains track overfitting. A small CNN (two conv blocks with batch norm and 2x2 max pooling, then two linear layers) is trained on Fashion-MNIST, validation split 10k, official test 10k, with plain SGD and with SAM (rho 0.1) for seeds 0, 1 and 2, under one pre-specified configuration: lr 0.03, momentum 0.9, weight decay 5e-4, batch 128, 60 epochs, cosine schedule. Both optimisers share the initialisation and data order per seed. Per-epoch training loss and validation ECE, NLL, Brier and adaptive calibration error, the top Hessian eigenvalue (power iteration, 20 iterations, fixed 2000-image batch) and the SAM rho-ball loss increase are in results/per_epoch.json. Test logits at epochs 5 and 60 feed test metrics and a temperature-scaling control fitted on validation only. Headline results at epoch 60 (descriptive, n=3): SAM test ECE 0.028 vs SGD 0.045 (mean difference -0.017, all seeds negative), SAM test NLL lower by 0.076, test accuracy difference +0.001 with mixed signs. Under temperature scaling the gap shrinks to 0.007 vs 0.009, so much of the advantage is a global confidence shift. SGD reaches training loss 0.0045 while SAM stays near 0.03. At matched training loss, SAM has a top Hessian eigenvalue about 12 to 16 lower and validation ECE about 0.010 to 0.014 lower. Epoch-5 calibration differences have no consistent sign. No p-values are reported; with 3 seed pairs no test can reach conventional significance. Files: method.py (training, unit checks, analysis entry), analysis.py, results/method_out.json and its full, mini and preview variants, results/notes.md, figures/ (six figures, PDF and PNG), and checks/ for independent re-derivation. Model weights and logits are kept on the run volume and are not in the published repository.
