## Layman Summary

Trains a small image classifier two ways and checks whether the sharpness-aware method makes its confidence scores more trustworthy without losing accuracy.

## Full Summary

Artifact: a controlled comparison of plain SGD (momentum 0.9) against sharpness-aware minimization (SAM) on a two-block CNN trained on Fashion-MNIST. Both optimizers share architecture, data split (50k/10k/10k from the 60k training images, official 10k test), batch size, schedule, weight decay and per-seed initialization and batch order. Learning rates (and SAM's rho) were chosen on validation NLL only, using seed 100. Final results use 5 paired seeds (0-4) at 40 epochs, plus compressed 5- and 15-epoch schedules to test whether the calibration gap grows with training length. A temperature-scaled SGD control is fitted on validation logits. Reported metrics: accuracy, ECE (15 equal-width bins), ACE, NLL, Brier, AURC, with paired t-intervals (df=4), Holm correction and reliability diagrams. Key results at epoch 40 (SAM minus SGD): accuracy +0.0015 with CI [-0.0007, +0.0036] (no detectable difference); ECE -0.0378 [-0.0414, -0.0341]; NLL -0.0980 [-0.1092, -0.0867]; Brier -0.0136; AURC -0.0015. Against temperature-scaled SGD, SAM's NLL is still lower (-0.0209 [-0.0266, -0.0151], Holm p=0.0027), but its ECE advantage is not significant after Holm (p=0.079). Caveats: SAM is under-fit at epoch 40 (training loss 0.107 against 0.008 for SGD), so part of its calibration advantage may come from a less-fit model; both selected learning rates (0.01) sit at the lower edge of the grid; one tuning configuration collapsed to chance (SAM lr 0.03, rho 0.2) and was not selected; the 60-epoch extension was not run. Scope is one dataset and one small architecture, with five seeds, so power is low. Independent re-derivation: the headline paired differences and CIs were recomputed directly from the saved test logits with separate code (explicit log-softmax and bin loops, scipy ttest_rel), and matched to the reported values; placebo checks (identical models, permuted labels) did not reject. Not re-derived: the ECE bin loop and ACE/Brier/AURC for every run were not independently recomputed, and the training itself was not repeated.
