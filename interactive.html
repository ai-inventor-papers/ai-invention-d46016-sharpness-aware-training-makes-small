      What the paper does not show

      • The main result is a lead: one tuned estimate, not specified in advance and not reproduced in an independent study.
      • Five paired seeds give low power. An exact sign-flip test cannot reach p below 0.05 with five seeds, so the reported p-values rely on the t-approximation.
      • NLL and ECE respond to confidence level even without label information, so the NLL result does not isolate learned structure.
      • The matched-loss check at the tuned SGD learning rate of 0.001 has not been run, and no 60-epoch extension was run.
      • The study covers one dataset and one small network. Larger networks are not tested.
