## Full Summary

Experiment artifact testing whether sharpness-aware minimisation (SAM) improves test accuracy and calibration over plain SGD for a small CNN on Fashion-MNIST, at matched training loss. Five paired seeds (0-4). Conditions: SAM for 40 epochs with rho=0.1 (chosen on validation NLL with seed 101), SGD for 40 epochs, and SGD for E*=10 epochs (chosen so its eval-mode training loss matches SAM's 40-epoch loss within 0.01, using seed 100). Every model is temperature-scaled on validation logits. Primary comparison: SAM 40 epochs minus matched-loss SGD. Headline differences (mean of 5 paired seeds, 95% t interval, df=4): test NLL raw -0.0125 [-0.0152, -0.0099], temperature-scaled NLL -0.0082 [-0.0110, -0.0053], raw ECE (15 equal-width bins) -0.0076 [-0.0091, -0.0061], accuracy +0.0025 [+0.0007, +0.0042]. Temperature-scaled ECE shows no difference. Holm correction across the 14-test family keeps raw ECE, raw NLL, temperature-scaled NLL, Brier and ACE; accuracy is not significant after correction (p=0.055). Secondary comparison (SAM vs SGD 40-epoch snapshot at the matched epoch) shows larger differences but is weaker because mid-schedule snapshots are not finished models. Caveats: five seeds give low power; the exact sign-flip test cannot reach p<0.05 with five seeds; a label-permutation placebo passes for accuracy but not for NLL and ECE, which reward lower confidence even with uninformative labels; E*=10 sits at the lower edge of the epoch grid; the SGD learning rate was fixed at 0.01 and not re-tuned. An independent re-derivation reproduces the primary headline numbers to within 1.2e-4. Outputs: method.py and analysis.py (pipeline and statistics), audit_rederive.py, results/method_out.json, mini and preview variants, reliability diagrams and a confidence histogram in figures/, and reproducibility.md with pinned dependencies in pyproject.toml.
