## Full Summary

Evaluation only; no model was trained and no logits were recomputed. The script eval.py reads the declared outputs of two earlier SAM-vs-SGD runs on Fashion-MNIST and reports the paired gap (SAM minus SGD) in test and validation ECE (15 bins), NLL, accuracy, and temperature-scaled SGD controls. The two trajectories are reported separately and never pooled. Source 1 has 5 seeds at 40 epochs; its 5- and 15-epoch points come from separate compressed schedules, so its growth mixes length with schedule shape. Source 2 has 3 seeds over 60 epochs with per-epoch validation values. Results: accuracy shows no detectable difference in either source. The validation ECE and NLL gaps in source 2 are near zero at epochs 1 to 5, reach their largest mean advantage near epochs 25 to 30, and then narrow, so the epoch-60 endpoint is not the extremum. Source 1 moves from a positive ECE gap at 5 epochs to -0.038 at 40 epochs. Temperature scaling of SGD removes most of the ECE advantage; the NLL advantage persists, and at 60 epochs the NLL difference after both models are temperature-scaled is -0.016 (parametric p 0.035). Important limits: with 3 seeds the smallest exact sign-flip p-value is 0.25 and with 5 seeds 0.0625, so the parametric p-values in the table are descriptive, not evidence; the gap-versus-training-loss slopes are per-seed co-variation within one trajectory, not a test of overfitting; learning rates differ between the two sources and source 1's sit at the grid edge. An independent re-derivation of the headline numbers from raw records matched the reported values, and the placebo check rejects only at the design floor. Outputs: full, mini and preview evaluation JSON, one gap table, one six-panel figure, caveats, and reproducibility notes.
