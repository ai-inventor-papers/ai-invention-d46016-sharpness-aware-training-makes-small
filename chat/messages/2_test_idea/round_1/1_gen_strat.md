**Strategy: "Tuned SAM vs SGD calibration on Fashion-MNIST"**

The strategy plans three artifacts, all with empty `depends_on` because this is the first iteration:

1. **Experiment, main matched comparison.** Learning-rate-tuned SGD against tuned SAM on a small CNN. Rho and learning rate are tuned on validation NLL only. Five paired seeds per optimizer, with saved validation and test logits at epochs 5, 15 and final. It also fits a per-model temperature on validation for the SGD runs, which is the control that separates a ranking gain from a global confidence shift.
2. **Experiment, mechanism check.** Three seeds per optimizer at a pre-specified default setting (not the tuned one, so the sharpness measurements are not selected on the outcome). It logs training loss each epoch and, every few epochs, ECE, NLL and top Hessian eigenvalue, to test whether the calibration gap tracks overfitting and sharpness.
3. **Research, prior-art check.** Confirms whether any study already compares SAM against temperature-scaled SGD on a small CNN, and identifies the baselines and rho ranges a reviewer would expect.

Paired statistics, bootstrap intervals, Holm correction and the final verdict are deferred to iteration 2, because they need this round's outputs. That deferral is recorded in `deferred_request_parts`.

**Choices I made that you may want to change**

- No domain handbook in the list fits ML calibration, so the domain reasoning rests on cited literature and is marked provisional.
- The tuning grid is small (SGD learning rate at three values; SAM learning rate at three values crossed with rho at three values), with a fallback to two learning rates if a run is slow. Weight decay is fixed at 5e-4 for both optimizers.
- Compute is estimated at about 3 GPU-hours for the main experiment and 2 for the mechanism check. These are rough figures I did not measure.
